## My impact this year

I am proud of the progress I helped deliver in 2026. I secured product ownership for CSR Chat, helped move it toward a faster and more broadly useful production experience, and established a clearer direction for the Compass platform. My contribution grew from individual product requirements and delivery coordination into connecting customer needs, engineering priorities, operational visibility, and commercial readiness across the portfolio.

The results are tangible. By September, CSR Chat supported **1,336 monthly active users and 32,114 human queries**. Monthly active users increased approximately **138% from May to September**, and September’s median response time was **11 seconds**. These are shared product and engineering outcomes that I am proud to have helped enable through focused priorities, customer alignment, and sustained delivery leadership.

## I helped turn CSR Chat progress into meaningful production outcomes

After transitioning my MHR/MEE responsibilities to Kent in February, I ramped into Minerva and took an increasingly active role in its roadmap, requirements, delivery coordination, and customer communication. By April, I was leading the daily delivery and incident discussions, helping align the Blue Shield of California roadmap, and establishing more consistent visibility into product performance.

Working with engineering and our cross-functional partners, I helped deliver three significant improvements:

- **A faster customer experience.** The performance release reduced median response time from 38 to 12 seconds and P95 from 79 to 25 seconds in the documented release comparison—approximately a 68% reduction. September’s 11-second median showed that the faster experience continued beyond the initial release window.
- **Broader benefit coverage.** Expansion into Large Group, Mid-Market, and Fully Insured business added approximately 5,400 plans and made the product available to approximately 300 additional CSRs.
- **Better operational visibility.** The Compass Performance Dashboard went live for Blue Shield of California on July 14, providing secure, role-based visibility into usage and product performance. This gave customers and internal teams a stronger basis for monitoring the experience and prioritizing improvements.

User survey feedback also showed the practical value of CSR Chat: **90% of respondents reported a positive impact when supporting benefit calls**, and **87% reported increased confidence answering benefits questions**. In addition, **86% said they trust the accuracy**, and **79% described the workflow fit as seamless or easy**. I am especially proud that respondents described value in their day-to-day work, greater confidence, and trust in the experience we delivered together.

I also deepened how I evaluated product success. I used the underlying usage data to examine active users, engagement, retention, and reacquisition, then translated those findings into leadership communications and the company-wide Stellarus Standup. This helped move our conversations beyond delivery activity toward how people were actually using the product.

Alongside the production work, I established the Compass Platform initiative to guide its evolution into a scalable, multi-tenant conversational AI platform. I helped organize the work needed to support reusable capabilities across experiences and customers, translating the vision into strategic objectives, delivery plans, capability roadmaps, and requirements covering tenant access, APIs and SDKs, orchestration, telemetry, and quality foundations.

I helped make the distinction between platform capabilities, product experiences, and customer deployments more explicit. That distinction matters as Compass expands: it creates a clearer basis for discussing shared investments, ownership, dependencies, and release readiness.

I also established a grounding taxonomy and structured use cases to inform the roadmap, and developed plans and requirements for golden datasets and customer onboarding. The platform and automated onboarding work remain in progress; my accomplishment to date is establishing the direction, requirements, and assessment needed to advance them responsibly.
