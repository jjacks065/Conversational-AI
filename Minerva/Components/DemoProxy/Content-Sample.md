**Brief Answer**  
Colonoscopy coverage depends on whether it is billed as a **preventive screening** or a **diagnostic/outpatient procedure**. Preventive screening is **No Charge** with an HMO Provider, while diagnostic colonoscopy-related outpatient professional and facility services generally apply at **35% coinsurance**, with some facility services subject to the medical deductible.

---

**Detailed Explanation**

Colonoscopy can be done as a preventive screening to detect potential issues before symptoms arise. These are referred as a screening and are covered under the Annual Health Exam benefits. They can also be done when there are specific symptoms or risk factors that warrant further investigation. These are known as a diagnostic procedures.

HMO PCP/ Medical Group coordination disclaimer:  
Because this is a HMO plan, the member must receive services from either an in-network provider or a provider associated with their medical group to be eligible for benefits. A referral from the member's primary care physician (PCP) is usually required when the member wants to see a specialist or other provider.

Access+: The patient does not need a referral for: Emergency Services; Urgent Services; Access+ Specialist visits; OB/GYN services by an obstetrician, gynecologist, or family practice Physician within the patient's Medical Group; and Office visits with the patient's primary care physician (PCP). The Access+ specialist visit includes: (1) An examination or consultation without referral from personal physician; (2) Conventional X-rays; (3) Laboratory Services; (4) Diagnostic or treatment procedures which a plan specialist would regularly provide under a referral.

### A. Preventive Screening Colonoscopy — Annual Health Exam

#### In-Network Provider
For in-network providers,

The plan will reimburse the provider 100% of the allowed amount for Annual Health Exams services. [1]

#### Out-of-Network Provider
For out-of-network providers, the member is responsible for 100% of the provider's billed amount, as the plan only covers in-network providers.

---

### B. Diagnostic Colonoscopy — Professional Services in an Outpatient Facility

#### In-Network Provider
For in-network providers,

The plan has a **$2,500 deductible per person**, not to exceed **$5,000 per family**.

However, Physician or surgeon services in an outpatient facility is not subject to the deductible.

The coinsurance is **35%** of the allowed amount, and the plan will reimburse the remaining **65%** of the allowed amount for Physician or surgeon services in an outpatient facility services. [2]

The service applies to the plan's out-of-pocket maximum amount of **$8,750 individual out-of-pocket maximum**, not to exceed **$17,500 family out-of-pocket maximum** per family. When the plan's out-of-pocket maximum amount is satisfied, the health plan will reimburse 100% of the allowed amount.

#### Out-of-Network Provider
For out-of-network providers, the member is responsible for 100% of the provider's billed amount, as the plan only covers in-network providers.

---

### C. Diagnostic Colonoscopy — Outpatient Hospital Facility Surgery

#### In-Network Provider
For in-network providers,

The plan has a **$2,500 deductible per person**, not to exceed **$5,000 per family**.

Hospital Services — Outpatient Surgeries is subject to this deductible. Once the deductible is met,

The coinsurance is **35%** of the allowed amount, and the plan will reimburse the remaining **65%** of the allowed amount for Hospital Services — Outpatient Surgeries services. [3]

The service applies to the plan's out-of-pocket maximum amount of **$8,750 individual out-of-pocket maximum**, not to exceed **$17,500 family out-of-pocket maximum** per family. When the plan's out-of-pocket maximum amount is satisfied, the health plan will reimburse 100% of the allowed amount.

Professional services disclaimer:  
In addition to the hospital bill, the member can expect related professional fees for services like surgery, anesthesia, and diagnostic testing (radiological, pathology, or laboratory).

#### Out-of-Network Provider
For out-of-network providers, the member is responsible for 100% of the provider's billed amount, as the plan only covers in-network providers.

### D. Diagnostic Colonoscopy — Ambulatory Surgery Center

#### In-Network Provider
For in-network providers,

The plan has a **$2,500 deductible per person**, not to exceed **$5,000 per family**.

Ambulatory Surgery Centers is subject to this deductible. Once the deductible is met,

The coinsurance is **35%** of the allowed amount, and the plan will reimburse the remaining **65%** of the allowed amount for Ambulatory Surgery Centers services. [4]

The service applies to the plan's out-of-pocket maximum amount of **$8,750 individual out-of-pocket maximum**, not to exceed **$17,500 family out-of-pocket maximum** per family. When the plan's out-of-pocket maximum amount is satisfied, the health plan will reimburse 100% of the allowed amount.

Surgery Disclaimer:  
In addition to the surgeon's and facility bill, the member can expect related fees for professional services like anesthesia or any related to diagnostic testing (e.g., radiological, pathology, or laboratory).

A surgical package includes the surgical procedure, pre-operative care medical visits on the same day of surgery and normal uncomplicated follow-up care.

#### Out-of-Network Provider
For out-of-network providers, the member is responsible for 100% of the provider's billed amount, as the plan only covers in-network providers.
