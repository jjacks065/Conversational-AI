## Executive Summary

The FEP Conversational AI Pilot has successfully completed its initial learning phase, providing **valuable insights and a clear roadmap for optimization**. The system demonstrates strong operational foundation with 24/7 availability and consistent volume handling, while revealing specific areas for targeted enhancement typical of AI startup implementations.

- **Resolution patterns identified** - 60% current rate with clear improvement pathways
- **Containment strategy refined** - 30% initial achievement with roadmap to 75% target
