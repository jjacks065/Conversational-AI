## 🎯 **1. AI Solution Overview**

### **Technology:** Sierra AI Conversational Agent Platform
- **Vendor:** Sierra AI (formerly ASAPP)  
- **Integration Partner:** Genesys (Contact Center Platform)
- **Deployment Model:** Cloud-based SaaS with on-premise data connectivity
- **AI Capabilities:** Natural Language Processing, Intent Recognition, Contextual Response Generation

### **Business Application**
- **Primary Use Case:** Customer service automation for routine member inquiries
- **Target Interactions:** Claims status, member benefits, FEP eligibility, provider verification
- **Expected Volume:** 75% of routine inquiries (~15,000 interactions/month)
- **Human Escalation:** Complex cases, member dissatisfaction, system limitations

## 🛡️ **2. AI Ethics & Responsible AI Framework**

### **Ethical Principles Implemented**
| Principle | Implementation | Validation Method |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Transparency** | Member notification of AI interaction, clear escalation paths | User experience testing, call monitoring |
| **Fairness** | Consistent service across all member demographics | Bias testing, outcome analysis by member segments |
| **Privacy** | PHI/PII protection, minimal data collection | Privacy impact assessment, audit trails |
| **Accountability** | Human oversight, audit logging, escalation procedures | Governance dashboards, monthly reviews |

- **Training Data Audits:** Vendor-provided bias testing across demographic groups
- **Outcome Monitoring:** Monthly analysis of service quality by member segments

## 🚨 **6. Incident Management & Response**

### **Escalation Matrix**
| Incident Severity | Response Time | Notification | Resolution Target |
|-------------------|---------------|--------------|------------------|
| **Critical** (System Down) | <15 minutes | C-Suite, Governance Chair | <2 hours |
| **High** (Data Breach) | <30 minutes | Legal, Compliance, Governance | <4 hours |
| **Medium** (Performance Degradation) | <1 hour | Operations Manager | <24 hours |
| **Low** (Minor Issues) | <4 hours | Technical Team | <72 hours |

### **Incident Response Procedures**
1. **Detection:** Automated monitoring + human oversight
2. **Assessment:** Severity classification, impact analysis
3. **Response:** Technical remediation, stakeholder notification
4. **Resolution:** System restoration, root cause analysis  
5. **Review:** Post-incident analysis, process improvement
