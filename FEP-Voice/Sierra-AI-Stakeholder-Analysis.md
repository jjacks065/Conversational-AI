# Sierra AI Conversational Agent - Stakeholder Analysis & Communication Plan

**Project:** Sierra AI Conversational Agent for BCBS Customer Service  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** March 16, 2026  
**Author:** Product Management  

## Executive Summary

This document identifies and analyzes all stakeholders involved in the Sierra AI conversational agent project, categorizing their influence, interest levels, and communication requirements to ensure successful project delivery and adoption.

## Stakeholder Categories

### 🔴 PRIMARY STAKEHOLDERS (High Influence, High Interest)

#### **1. Blue Cross Blue Shield Association (BCBSA)**
- **Role:** Primary client and project sponsor
- **Key Interests:** Cost reduction, operational efficiency, member satisfaction, regulatory compliance
- **Influence Level:** HIGH - Final decision authority and budget approval
- **Communication Needs:** Weekly executive updates, milestone reviews, risk escalations
- **Key Contacts:** Executive leadership, procurement team
- **Success Criteria:** Achievement of cost savings and operational metrics

#### **2. Blue Shield California (BSC) - Pilot Site**  
- **Role:** Primary implementation partner and pilot location
- **Key Interests:** Successful pilot execution, operational readiness, customer service improvement
- **Influence Level:** HIGH - Implementation success dependent on their participation
- **Communication Needs:** Daily operational updates, training coordination, feedback loops
- **Key Contacts:** Operations managers, customer service supervisors, IT administrators
- **Success Criteria:** Smooth pilot launch, positive customer feedback, operational KPI achievement

#### **3. Sierra AI / ASAPP**
- **Role:** Technology vendor and solution provider
- **Key Interests:** Successful deployment, platform performance, contract expansion
- **Influence Level:** HIGH - Solution delivery and technical performance responsibility
- **Communication Needs:** Real-time technical status, integration support, performance monitoring
- **Key Contacts:** Technical delivery team, customer success managers, engineering support
- **Success Criteria:** Platform performance SLAs, successful integration, customer satisfaction

#### **4. Customer Service Representatives**
- **Role:** End users who will work alongside AI agent and handle escalations
- **Key Interests:** Job security, training adequacy, workflow efficiency, performance support
- **Influence Level:** MEDIUM-HIGH - User adoption critical for success
- **Communication Needs:** Training schedules, operational procedures, feedback channels
- **Key Contacts:** Team leads, union representatives, training coordinators
- **Success Criteria:** Smooth workflow integration, maintained job satisfaction, skill development

### 🟡 SECONDARY STAKEHOLDERS (Medium Influence, High Interest)

#### **5. BCBS Members (Standard & FEP)**
- **Role:** End beneficiaries of improved customer service
- **Key Interests:** Faster service, accurate information, seamless experience
- **Influence Level:** MEDIUM - Customer satisfaction drives project success
- **Communication Needs:** Service announcements, feature education, feedback collection
- **Key Contacts:** Member services, communications team
- **Success Criteria:** Improved service experience, reduced wait times, high satisfaction scores

#### **6. Federal Employee Program (FEP) Administration**
- **Role:** Specialized member segment with unique requirements
- **Key Interests:** FEP-specific functionality, compliance with federal requirements
- **Influence Level:** MEDIUM - Specialized requirements must be met
- **Communication Needs:** FEP-specific updates, compliance confirmations, testing coordination
- **Key Contacts:** FEP operations team, compliance officers
- **Success Criteria:** Full FEP feature compatibility, regulatory compliance

#### **7. BCBS IT & Technology Teams**
- **Role:** Internal technical support and system integration
- **Key Interests:** System stability, security, integration success, maintenance procedures
- **Influence Level:** MEDIUM - Critical for technical implementation
- **Communication Needs:** Technical specifications, security reviews, deployment plans
- **Key Contacts:** IT managers, security teams, integration specialists
- **Success Criteria:** Secure integration, system stability, documented procedures

#### **8. Healthcare Providers**
- **Role:** External users accessing member information
- **Key Interests:** Accurate eligibility information, efficient verification process
- **Influence Level:** MEDIUM - Provider satisfaction impacts member experience
- **Communication Needs:** System changes, new capabilities, training materials
- **Key Contacts:** Provider relations, EDI teams
- **Success Criteria:** Maintained or improved provider experience, accurate data

### 🟢 TERTIARY STAKEHOLDERS (Variable Influence, Medium Interest)

#### **9. Compliance & Legal Teams**
- **Role:** Regulatory compliance oversight and risk management
- **Key Interests:** HIPAA compliance, data protection, audit trail, regulatory alignment
- **Influence Level:** MEDIUM - Can halt project for compliance issues
- **Communication Needs:** Compliance reports, audit documentation, risk assessments
- **Key Contacts:** Chief compliance officer, legal counsel, audit team
- **Success Criteria:** Full regulatory compliance, documented audit trails

#### **10. Genesys (Technology Partner)**
- **Role:** Contact center platform integration partner
- **Key Interests:** Successful platform integration, partnership maintenance
- **Influence Level:** LOW-MEDIUM - Integration dependency
- **Communication Needs:** Technical coordination, integration timelines, testing support
- **Key Contacts:** Technical account managers, integration engineers
- **Success Criteria:** Seamless platform integration, maintained partnership

#### **11. Other Blues Plans (Phase 2)**
- **Role:** Future implementation candidates and expansion stakeholders  
- **Key Interests:** Pilot results, scalability proof, implementation roadmap
- **Influence Level:** LOW - Future expansion dependent on pilot success
- **Communication Needs:** Pilot results sharing, lessons learned, expansion planning
- **Key Contacts:** Operations leadership, technology teams
- **Success Criteria:** Positive pilot results, clear expansion pathway

#### **12. Internal Audit**
- **Role:** Independent verification and governance oversight
- **Key Interests:** Process compliance, risk management, control effectiveness
- **Influence Level:** MEDIUM - Can require changes for compliance
- **Communication Needs:** Audit schedules, process documentation, control testing
- **Key Contacts:** Internal audit team, risk management
- **Success Criteria:** Successful audit outcomes, controlled risk profile

## Stakeholder Communication Matrix

| Stakeholder Group | Frequency | Method | Key Messages | Escalation Path |
|------------------|-----------|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| BCBSA Leadership | Weekly | Executive dashboards | Progress, risks, metrics | Project sponsor |
| BSC Operations | Daily | Team meetings, Slack | Operational status, issues | Operations manager |
| Sierra/ASAPP | As needed | Technical calls | Development, support | Account manager |
| Customer Service Reps | Weekly | Town halls, training | Updates, procedures | Team supervisors |
| Members | As needed | Communications | Service improvements | Member services |
| IT Teams | Bi-weekly | Technical reviews | Integration, security | IT manager |
| Compliance | Monthly | Compliance reports | Risk status, controls | Chief compliance officer |

## Risk Mitigation by Stakeholder

| Stakeholder | Primary Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|-------------|--------------|-------------------|
| BCBSA | Project ROI not achieved | Clear success metrics, regular reporting |
| BSC Operations | Pilot disruption | Comprehensive training, phased rollout |
| Sierra/ASAPP | Technical delivery delays | Clear SLAs, regular check-ins |
| Customer Service Reps | Job displacement fears | Reskilling programs, clear career paths |
| Members | Service degradation | Extensive testing, gradual rollout |
| Compliance | Regulatory violations | Early compliance reviews, audit trails |

## Key Stakeholder Meetings & Touchpoints

- **Weekly Executive Steering Committee** (BCBSA, BSC leadership)
- **Daily Operations Standups** (BSC operations, Sierra technical team)  
- **Bi-weekly Technical Reviews** (IT teams, Sierra engineering)
- **Monthly Compliance Reviews** (Legal, compliance, audit)
- **Quarterly Business Reviews** (All primary stakeholders)
