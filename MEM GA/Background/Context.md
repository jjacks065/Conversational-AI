**Benefits Quoting via Sierra** is a member‑facing conversational AI that resolves routine benefit questions through voice and digital self‑service, reducing live‑agent demand while delivering fast, accurate, and accessible answers at scale

**Product:** Sierra AI – Benefits Quoting Agent

**Primary End Users:** Members

**Economic Buyers:** Member Experience, Digital Experience, and Contact Center C‑suite

**Interaction Modes:** Voice, chat, and digital messaging (omnichannel)

Product Description

**Benefits Quoting via Sierra is a member‑facing conversational AI designed to resolve routine benefit inquiries through self‑service across voice and digital channels.** It enables members to ask natural‑language questions about their benefits and receive immediate, policy‑grounded responses without requiring live agent intervention.

Sierra is optimized for **containment, accessibility, and scalability** by handling high‑volume, lower‑complexity benefit questions while escalating edge cases to live agents when needed. By resolving common inquiries upfront, Sierra reduces inbound call volume, shortens wait times, and lowers cost‑to‑serve, while improving member satisfaction.

During pilots, Sierra demonstrated strong performance in self‑service containment and issue resolution, validating its role as the front door of the AI‑Driven Member Advocacy Hub.

Strategic Value

* **Digital containment:** Deflects a meaningful share of benefit inquiries from live agents
* **Always‑on access:** 24/7 availability across voice and digital channels
* **Cost efficiency:** Lower marginal cost per resolved inquiry compared to agent‑assisted interactions
* **Experience lift:** Faster answers and reduced friction improve member satisfaction
