03 · Default milestone order

Six complete capability increments.

The baseline order compounds learning—from grounded answers and contextual authorization to high-assurance transactional actions.

Baseline sequence: value first, then progressively richer context and action assurance.Capability units may move as a whole.
1

Eligibility

Resolve coverage status, effective dates, product and plan context, and key restrictions for the right person at the right point in time.

Hardensactor-versus-subject authorization · provider access · point-in-time context
2

Claims

Explain claim status, adjudication, member responsibility, denial context, and appropriate next steps across multiple records.

HardensPHI minimization · record clarification · code interpretation and explainability
3

Accumulators

Ground deductible and out-of-pocket balances by individual or family, network, benefit period, and authoritative as-of date.

Hardensfinancial precision · effective periods · deterministic reconciliation
4

Formulary

Clarify drug coverage, tier, prior authorization, step therapy, and quantity limits without crossing clinical decision boundaries.

Hardensentity normalization · policy versioning · clinical safety boundaries
5

Provider Lookup

Find and rank relevant providers using specialty, location, network, accessibility, and fresh directory context.

Hardenssearch and ranking · source freshness · stable ProviderReference
6

Provider Scheduling

Find availability and safely book, reschedule, or cancel with explicit confirmation and transparent action status.

Hardensidempotency · audit and compensation · concurrency and transactional control
04 · Release discipline

Quality is shared. Release is independent.

Common gates protect members and operations; versioned contracts prevent one capability’s sequence from becoming another capability’s dependency.
