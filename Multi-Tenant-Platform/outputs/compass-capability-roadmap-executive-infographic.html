Page 2 · Resolution quality drill-down

Grounded Resolution Rate

A resolution must be earned—not declared.

GRR measures whether Compass resolved an eligible healthcare inquiry correctly, completely, and usefully with trusted evidence. The system outcome starts the review; independent adjudication proves it.

≥95% Release target on eligible, source-adjudicated inquiry episodes—with zero critical unsupported factual claims.
NumeratorEligible inquiry episodes that end in a verified grounded resolution
÷
DenominatorAll eligible inquiry episodes—including product-caused non-resolutions
01 · Metric boundary

Fix eligibility. Then judge the result.

Eligibility is assigned independently of the agent’s answer. Difficult cases stay visible; successful answers must clear every grounding gate.

Denominator · what is eligible

A supported inquiry Compass is expected to handle

Eligibility is determined from the request and product contract—not from whether the system succeeded.

✓Intent maps to an enabled capability and supported journey.
✓Surface, tenant, cohort, persona, and language are supported.
✓Actor and subject can be authorized, including through reasonable clarification.
✓An authoritative source is expected to contain the required information.
Outages, retrieval failures, unnecessary escalation, and post-engagement abandonment remain in the denominator.
Numerator · what passes

A verified resolution—not a self-reported outcome

The episode may count only after an independent source-and-trace review confirms every required condition.

✓The final episode outcome is resolved_grounded.
✓Material facts match the authoritative source and applicable as-of date.
✓The response addresses the supported objective without material omissions.
✓Evidence, provenance, freshness, limitations, and presentation are sufficient.
Clarification can occur within an episode; a clarification-only, abstained, escalated, or failed episode is not a grounded resolution.
02 · Resolution standard

Five gates. All must pass.

GRR is intentionally all-or-nothing at the episode level. Partial scores diagnose quality; they do not inflate this resolution-quality measure.

Gate 01

Context

Correct tenant, actor, subject, authorization, capability, and applicable dates.

Gate 02

Correctness

Every material fact agrees with the authoritative source or approved deterministic rule.

Gate 03

Completeness

The supported objective is answered without a material omission that changes meaning.

Gate 04

Grounding

Source, provenance, freshness/as-of, and relevant limitations remain traceable.

Gate 05

Usability

The answer is understandable, actionable, and factually equivalent across surface variants.

Automatic failure:Any critical unsupported claim, wrong-subject result, incorrect amount or status, or stale answer represented as current.
03 · Unit of measure

Score the inquiry episode—not each turn.

One inquiry ID follows the user objective through clarification and resolution, preventing normal multi-turn behavior from distorting performance.

04 · Measurement system

One metric. Three evidence layers.

Release evidence proves readiness, production adjudication measures real performance, and continuous instrumentation finds problems quickly.

Layer 01 · Release gate

Evaluation GRR

A frozen, source-labeled and risk-balanced scenario set measures launch readiness and regression.

Coverage ≥100 scenarios per capability
Cadence Every material build and release
Decision Must meet ≥95% before cohort launch
Layer 02 · Hero report

Production-adjudicated GRR

A stratified random sample is reviewed against source records, traces, policy, and the delivered surface response.

Sampling Random + risk oversample
Cadence Weekly; rolling 30-day view
Statistics Traffic-weighted rate, n, and 95% confidence interval
Layer 03 · Early signal

Operational grounding proxy

Automated checks cover every inquiry for context, schema, source, freshness, provenance, and declared outcome.

Coverage 100% of instrumented traffic
Cadence Near real time
Constraint Diagnostic proxy—not published as verified GRR
