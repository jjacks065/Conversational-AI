# Compass Platform Capability Roadmap — Infographic Brief

## Audience and executive decision

- Audience: product, technology, operations, and executive leaders aligning on Compass delivery.
- Decision: fund and govern Compass as a shared capability platform whose milestones ship as complete, independently operable product increments.

## One-sentence takeaway

Compass turns Member, CSR, and Provider conversations into grounded resolutions by connecting governed surfaces through a shared Spine to trusted Brain services—expanding one whole healthcare capability at a time.

## Narrative arc

1. Establish the product promise and north-star outcome.
2. Show the from-to: surface-specific conversational work becomes reusable, governed capability delivery.
3. Define the shared platform architecture: Surfaces → Spine → Brain.
4. Define what “whole” means for every capability milestone.
5. Present the six capability release units in their default order.
6. Close with common exit gates and the rules that preserve reorderability.

## Required content

- North star: Grounded Resolution Rate (GRR), with Safe Handling Rate as the complementary guardrail.
- Current-to-target movement, including CSR Chat converging as a Compass surface.
- Architecture responsibilities:
  - Surfaces: persona, presentation, and experience policy.
  - Spine: auth, Agent Broker, capability agents, state, policy, actions, and telemetry.
  - Brain: governed knowledge and data services with provenance and freshness.
- Whole-capability delivery contract: product scope, personas, authorization, grounded sources, runtime, routing, structured response, configurable variants, safe fallbacks, governed actions, typed outcomes, evaluation, and operations.
- Ordered milestones: Eligibility, Claims, Accumulators, Formulary, Provider Lookup, Provider Scheduling.
- Independence rules and common quality gates.

## Visual hierarchy

- Hero: promise + architecture.
- Large metric: GRR ≥95% target, paired with Safe Handling ≥99% target.
- Roadmap: six numbered capability cards; product value first, platform hardening second.
- Footer: “Sequence can change. Product integrity cannot.”

## Tone

Executive, ambitious, grounded, and specific. Avoid implementation-level migration detail, speculative dates, or dependency chains between healthcare capabilities.
