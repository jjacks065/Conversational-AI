# Compass Platform Capability Roadmap — Infographic Brief

## Audience and executive decision

- Audience: product, technology, operations, and executive leaders aligning on Compass delivery.
- Decision: position Member Journey as the M0 foundation and the horizontal acceptance lens for whole, independently operable capability increments. Rebaseline the existing PRDs and estimates before delivery commitment.

## One-sentence takeaway

Compass earns trust through grounded answers, then becomes a member-directed Personal Health Assistant across concurrent care journeys. The Member Journey foundation begins at M0 and matures through every whole capability increment; C6 demonstrates a specialist visit plus medication end to end.

## Narrative arc

1. Establish the member-directed product promise and top-line Member Progress + Earned Trust outcomes.
2. Show the from-to: inquiry/capability-led work becomes continuous, member-owned care journeys across surfaces.
3. Define the shared platform architecture: Surfaces → Spine → Brain.
4. Define the horizontal Member Journey loop and its M0 floor.
5. Present the six whole capability release units in their default order, culminating in the C6 specialist-plus-medication signature journey.
6. Close with common quality gates, member control, and PRD/engineering rebaseline needs. Keep page 2 as the GRR quality-metric drill-down.

## Required content

- Top-line outcomes: Member Progress and Earned Trust. GRR ≥95% and Safe Handling ≥99% remain the existing quality/safety release targets; journey-outcome baselines and targets are not yet available.
- Multiple concurrent member-owned journeys; member chooses engagement, goals, cadence, accepted future steps, and closure. Member attestation advances progress; external evidence updates provenance without rewriting history.
- Direct, member-authorized payer/provider coordination where available; guided contact fallback; human intervention clears specific blockers without taking over coordination; provider and member direct treatment.
- Current-to-target movement, including CSR Chat converging as a Compass surface.
- Architecture responsibilities:
  - Surfaces: persona, presentation, and experience policy.
  - Spine: auth, Agent Broker, capability agents, state, policy, actions, and telemetry.
  - Brain: governed knowledge and data services with provenance and freshness.
- Whole-capability delivery contract: product scope, personas, authorization, grounded sources, runtime, routing, structured response, configurable variants, safe fallbacks, governed actions, typed outcomes, evaluation, and operations.
- Ordered milestones: Eligibility, Claims, Accumulators, Formulary, Provider Lookup, Provider Scheduling.
- Independence rules and common quality gates.

## Visual hierarchy

- Hero: member-directed companion promise + architecture.
- Large metric: Progress + Trust, with GRR ≥95% and Safe Handling ≥99% as quality/safety gates.
- Journey loop and M0 foundation band before six numbered capability cards; product value first, platform hardening second.
- Footer: “Sequence can change. Journey integrity cannot.”

## Tone

Executive, ambitious, grounded, and specific. Avoid implementation-level migration detail, speculative dates, or dependency chains between healthcare capabilities.
