THE VISION
Compass recognizes intent, deflects, and routes
Compass owns the conversation: it hears the member, understands them, and records every call.
1 · Recognize intent
Compass turns speech into text and 
works out the member's intent from 
what they say, not from a menu option 
or line of business.
2 · Deflect
Well-defined, high-volume requests 
are answered by AI agents, using plan 
data through one secure gateway, so 
they never reach a CSR queue.
3 · Route with context
Everything else goes to the right team, 
with the intent and a summary passed 
to the CSR through Shield Advisor.
Replaces Pega AI over time
Intent detection, summaries and suggestions move from Pega AI to Compass 
as each stage proves itself. Pega's role in cases and workflow is decided with 
its roadmap.
One offering, not more tools
Inbound voice and chat, agent assist and, later, outreach run on one platform 
with one view of member intent.
Compass doesn't replace the contact center. It replaces the guesswork about where a call should go.
