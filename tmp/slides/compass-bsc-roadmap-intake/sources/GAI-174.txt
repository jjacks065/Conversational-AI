Overall Summary:
The AI Use Case Risk Questionnaire identifies this Voice AI implementation as Medium risk, driven primarily by the presence of PHI, reliance on vendor cloud‑based processing, and the early‑stage maturity of operational controls. The use case remains assistive—not decision‑making—and includes human review of all summaries, reducing safety and fairness risk areas while still requiring appropriate safeguards.
Recommended Mitigations:
To manage a Medium‑risk profile, the team should focus on strengthening documentation, enhancing monitoring and drift/bias checks, and ensuring formal security validation prior to scaling beyond pilot. Additional emphasis should be placed on hardening vendor integrations, confirming HIPAA‑aligned safeguards, and maturing operational oversight so the solution can safely progress from pilot to wider deployment.
