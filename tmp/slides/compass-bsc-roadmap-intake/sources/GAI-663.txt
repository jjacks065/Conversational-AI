AI Capabilities / Usage:
The proposed solution leverages Generative AI capabilities within Pega (Coach AI and Knowledge/Content Buddy) to enhance agent productivity and customer interactions.
Generative AI is used to:
Generate real-time responses to agent queries using natural language, grounded in enterprise knowledge sources
Summarize customer interactions, cases, and historical data to provide quick context and reduce manual effort
Recommend relevant knowledge articles and next-best actions during live member calls
Draft responses and assist with communication, improving consistency and reducing response time
Automate routine tasks such as case summaries, follow-ups, and documentation

Description
This user story focuses on implementing Pega Knowledge (Content Buddy) and Coach AI capabilities within Shield Advisor (Pega) to improve agent efficiency and customer experience.
The solution will enable:
Real-time agent guidance using natural language responses during member calls
Intelligent retrieval of knowledge articles and process information
Faster navigation of information lookup tools and reduced manual research effort
This aligns with the business goal of improving agent productivity, reducing training time, and enhancing member interaction quality.
Problem Statement
Agents currently face delays in:
Training for sentiment-based interactions
Navigating knowledge articles
Researching member inquiries
Accessing benefit quoting and lookup tools
This impacts operational efficiency and multiple business metrics.
Business Value / Expected Outcomes
Reduced average handle time (AHT)
Improved first call resolution (FCR)
Faster agent onboarding and training
Enhanced customer experience through real-time assistance
Reduced manual effort for information lookup

Editing previous assessment based on additional information provided by the Shawna Maitia  and Hina Khan
This use case is Medium Risk. This is an advisory rating and does not approve/deny the use case. Per question support available: https://blueshieldca.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/ISRM/pages/1917255764/AI+Risk+Assessment+Process+Support+Guide
Overall Risk Profile:
The rating is driven by use of generative AI in agent-facing member service workflows, third-party Pega capabilities, regulated health plan operations, and potential member impact through guidance, searches, summaries, and recommendations. Risk is reduced by the requestor’s clarification that Knowledge and Coach do not access, store, or save PHI or PII, Knowledge retrieves procedural SMP articles, Coach operates on configured business rules and masked case context, prompts are not used for model training, and BSC controls data exposure, knowledge sources, user access, and partner access.
Recommended Mitigation Actions:
To reduce the risk, document the confirmed PHI and PII exclusion design, identify the exact data fields and knowledge sources available to Knowledge and Coach, maintain masking and role-based access controls, confirm prompt and output retention settings, complete contract and governance approvals, validate security and resilience controls, and establish monitoring for accuracy, relevance, compliance adherence, access violations, and inappropriate data exposure.

Live call audio - No

Interaction notes - Potentially, if interaction notes are stored within the host application and intentionally provided as case context to Coach. GenAI Coach is designed to provide guidance based on application and case data.
Interaction summaries - Not out of the box.  If BSC so chooses, you could add these as a knowledge source or tool for Coach later on if it is a requirement for the Coach to use this info.
Prompts used for model training, tuning, or improvement - No, customer prompts submitted to GenAI Coach are not used for model training, tuning, or product improvement. Coach receives its prompt instruction from within Infinity Studio through Pega.  Additionally, the Coach can be contained to only use information contained within Pega or your content/data behind the firewall and not be exposed to the internet.
If yes to any of the above, please confirm whether the data contains any PHI, PII, member-specific information, claims, benefits, or other identifiable member data. If the data is anonymized, de-identified, or limited to procedural/business-rule content only, please confirm that as well. - This data can be masked (configured in the data portion of Infinity Studio) and will only be available to Coach in its masked form.
Also, please describe what controls prevent PHI/PII from being provided to Coach, and whether Pega or any partner has rights to use prompts or related data beyond BSC’s controlled purpose. - You maintain access to which case data is made available to the Coach, which knowledge sources it can see and which user populations can access the coaching capabilities and what is exposed to end users.
In summary, GenAI Coach is a case guidance and workflow coaching capability. Based on the information reviewed, it is not designed to process live call audio, call recordings, or transcription streams. Coach operates on application and case context configured by the BSC and therefore only accesses member, claims, benefits, PHI, or PII information if such data is intentionally made available through the hosting application. This is a great source for additional trust and security information http://www.pega.com/trust.
The only way Pega and Partner access would be granted to view your data would be based on roles BSC provides to those respective individuals to the respective applications.
