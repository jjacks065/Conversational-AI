Compass CSR Chat
Product Brief
Prepared by Stellarus  |  November 2026
Executive summary
Compass CSR Chat helps health plan customer service representatives answer benefit questions during 
live member and provider interactions. Representatives ask questions in natural language and receive 
structured, plan-specific responses grounded in approved benefit data. The product helps bring coverage 
details, cost sharing, conditions, and required disclosure language into a consistent workflow for 
representative review.
Current product scope
The core product supports representative-assisted benefit inquiry: locating relevant benefit information, 
explaining plan terms, and presenting the applicable conditions together. It includes answer guardrails, 
representative feedback, and operational monitoring. Plan coverage and enabled features depend on the 
data, configuration, and validation completed for each customer.
Value for health plans
CSR Chat is intended to reduce the effort required to find and interpret benefit information and improve 
consistency across representatives. Each health plan establishes its own baseline and success measures for 
answer quality, required disclosures, representative adoption, and benefit-inquiry handling. Results 
depend on source quality, workflow design, and adoption.
Responsible adoption
Representatives retain responsibility for reviewing responses, following approved procedures, and 
communicating with the customer. Additional information domains and delivery options require 
confirmation for the agreed implementation. CSR Chat does not independently determine member 
eligibility, adjudicate claims, or make authoritative coverage decisions.
