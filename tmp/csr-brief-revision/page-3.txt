1. Product Overview
Compass CSR Chat is an AI-powered benefit-inquiry assistant for health plan customer service teams. It 
combines plan-specific information with a controlled response workflow so representatives can find, 
interpret, and explain approved benefit details during a live interaction.
1.1 How CSR Chat works
A request includes the applicable customer and plan context from the approved experience. CSR Chat 
retrieves relevant benefit information and prepares a structured response for review. A question such as 
"What cost sharing applies to an in-network specialist visit?" can bring together the available copay or 
coinsurance, deductible rules, and relevant conditions. The representative checks the response before 
communicating it.

1.3 Users and customer configuration
Representatives use CSR Chat for day-to-day benefit inquiries; supervisors and benefit reviewers support 
quality and workflow improvement. Health plans agree terminology, answer presentation, disclosure 
language, and supported plan data with Stellarus. Configuration is subject to the approved implementation 
and validation process.
1.4 Product scope and boundaries
Supported answers depend on the plan data integrated and validated for the customer. Plan-level 
deductible rules do not establish a member's remaining balances or eligibility. The baseline is 
representative-assisted benefit inquiry; claims, eligibility, live accumulators, formulary, and provider 
navigation require separately confirmed scope and readiness. The representative retains final 
communication responsibility.
