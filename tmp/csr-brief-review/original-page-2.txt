1. Product Snapshot
Compass CSR Chat is an AI-powered decision-support assistant for health plan customer service 
representatives. During a live interaction, a representative asks a benefitt question in natural language and 
receives a structured, plan-specifitc answer grounded in the health plan's approved benefitt data.
Product promise
Help representatives reach accurate, consistent benefitt information faster while keeping the 
representative responsible for the fitnal customer communication.
1.1 What Compass CSR Chat is
Compass CSR Chat brings benefitt interpretation into the representative workfloow. It retrieves the plan 
context supplied with the request, identifites relevant benefitt information, and generates an answer using that 
context. When the available context does not support an answer, the product is designed to say that it 
cannot answer rather than rely on general knowledge.

1.3 Product scope
Thee current product scope is representative-assisted benefitt inquiry. Supported plan populations depend on 
which benefitt data has been integrated and validated for the health plan. Additional domains are enabled 
only afteer their source data, rules, and validation are complete.
 Agent-facing: Compass CSR Chat supports representatives; it is not a member-facing chatbot.
 Decision support: It assists with benefitt questions; it is not a system of record or claims adjudication 
system.
 Plan-grounded: It answers from approved customer context; it is not permitteed to answer from general 
knowledge.
