[033] Heading1 | 1. Product Snapshot
[034]  | Compass CSR Chat is an AI-powered decision-support assistant for health plan customer service representatives. During a live interaction, a representative asks a benefit question in natural language and receives a structured, plan-specific answer grounded in the health plan's approved benefit data.
[035]  | Product promise
[036]  | Help representatives reach accurate, consistent benefit information faster while keeping the representative responsible for the final customer communication.
[037] Heading2 | 1.1 What Compass CSR Chat is
[038]  | Compass CSR Chat brings benefit interpretation into the representative workflow. It retrieves the plan context supplied with the request, identifies relevant benefit information, and generates an answer using that context. When the available context does not support an answer, the product is designed to say that it cannot answer rather than rely on general knowledge.

[055] Heading2 | 1.3 Product scope
[056]  | The current product scope is representative-assisted benefit inquiry. Supported plan populations depend on which benefit data has been integrated and validated for the health plan. Additional domains are enabled only after their source data, rules, and validation are complete.
[057] ListBullet | Agent-facing: Compass CSR Chat supports representatives; it is not a member-facing chatbot.
[058] ListBullet | Decision support: It assists with benefit questions; it is not a system of record or claims adjudication system.
[059] ListBullet | Plan-grounded: It answers from approved customer context; it is not permitted to answer from general knowledge.
[060] Heading1 | 2. Problem and Value
[061] Heading2 | 2.1 The customer service challenge
[062]  | Benefit questions often require representatives to move across plan documents, internal tools, and policy sources while a member or provider waits. Representatives must interpret specialized language, identify the correct plan details, and communicate required conditions consistently.
[063]  | Challenge
[064]  | Operational effect
[065]  | Manual navigation across sources
[066]  | Longer time to locate and interpret the relevant benefit detail
[067]  | Complex plan language
[068]  | Dependence on individual experience and benefit literacy
[069]  | Inconsistent interpretation
[070]  | Variation in responses, disclosures, and escalation behavior
[071]  | Context switching
[072]  | Higher representative effort and reduced confidence during live calls
[073]  | Limited visibility
[074]  | Difficulty identifying recurring gaps and improvement opportunities
[075] Heading2 | 2.2 How Compass CSR Chat helps
[076]  | Compass CSR Chat combines the health plan's approved benefit data with a controlled answer-generation workflow. The representative asks a question, the application retrieves relevant plan context, and the model produces a structured response for representative review. Customer-specific configuration controls terminology, presentation, and required disclosure language.
[077] Heading2 | 2.3 Expected outcomes
[078] ListBullet | Faster access to benefit information during live interactions.
[079] ListBullet | More consistent interpretation and presentation across representatives.
[080] ListBullet | Greater representative confidence when handling complex benefit questions.
[081] ListBullet | A repeatable validation and feedback loop for improving answer quality.
[082] ListBullet | A scalable foundation for additional plans and approved information domains.
[083]  | Measurement approach
[084]  | Outcome targets are baselined with each health plan. Typical measures include time to answer, answer accuracy, required-disclosure compliance, representative adoption, feedback, and benefit-inquiry handle time. Results are not guaranteed and depend on implementation scope, source quality, workflow design, and adoption.

[145] Heading1 | 4. Use Cases
[146] Heading2 | 4.1 Benefit coverage
[147]  | A representative asks whether a service is covered under the applicable plan. Compass CSR Chat returns the available plan-specific coverage detail and relevant conditions for review.
[148]  | Illustrative question: “Is physical therapy covered under this plan?”
[149] Heading2 | 4.2 Cost sharing and conditions
[150]  | A representative asks about copays, coinsurance, deductibles, visit limits, network tiers, or other conditions contained in the loaded benefit data.
[151]  | Illustrative question: “What cost sharing applies to an in-network specialist visit?”
[152] Heading2 | 4.3 Plan interpretation
[153]  | A representative asks for the applicable benefit language to be summarized in a consistent, customer-approved answer structure, including relevant limitations or disclosure language.
[154]  | Illustrative question: “What limitations apply to this durable medical equipment benefit?”
[155]  | Use-case boundary
[156]  | Illustrative questions do not guarantee that every plan or information domain is available. A use case is supported only when the required source data and rules are in scope and have passed customer validation.

[188] Heading1 | 6. Responsible Use and Boundaries
[189] ListBullet | The representative remains responsible for following approved procedures and communicating the final answer.
[190] ListBullet | Compass CSR Chat does not replace formal plan documents, legal determinations, claims adjudication, or coverage decisions.
[191] ListBullet | The calling application supplies the applicable customer and plan context; Compass CSR Chat does not independently identify a member or determine which plan applies.
[192] ListBullet | Questions outside the available approved context are not answered.
