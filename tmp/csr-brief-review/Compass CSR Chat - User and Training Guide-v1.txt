1. Purpose, Audience, and Document Boundaries
Compass CSR Chat is an AI-powered decision-support assistant for benefit inquiries. It helps representatives retrieve and interpret plan-specific benefit information during live customer interactions. This guide establishes a safe, repeatable way to use the product without replacing customer procedures, authoritative sources, or human judgment.
Core user principle
CSR Chat supports the representative. The representative remains responsible for confirming the applicable context, reviewing the complete response, following required procedures, and communicating the final answer.

1.1 Intended audience
Authorized customer service representatives who use CSR Chat during member or provider interactions.
Supervisors, coaches, and trainers who prepare users and reinforce expected behavior.
Quality and benefit reviewers who assess answer use and provide feedback.
1.2 User responsibility
Use CSR Chat only for approved business purposes and supported benefit inquiries.
Confirm that the correct customer and plan context is active before asking a question.
Review the full response and apply required validation before communicating it.
Use an approved source or escalation path when the answer is missing, unclear, conflicting, or appears incorrect.
Protect PHI and PII according to the health plan's policies and approved product procedures.

2.2 Information and context
CSR Chat answers from approved benefit data associated with the plan context supplied for the request. The calling application supplies that context; CSR Chat does not select a member or determine which plan applies.
Plan identifiers determine which benefit data is available.
Effective dates may determine which plan version applies.
Available answers depend on the plans and information domains loaded and validated for the deployment.
CSR Chat is not permitted to answer unsupported questions from general knowledge.
Customer-specific rule
The health plan's operating procedure defines which member, case, plan, and effective-date context must be confirmed before a question is asked. That procedure takes precedence over general examples in this guide.

5.2 Before customer communication
Mandatory review
Do not communicate a CSR Chat response until you have reviewed it and completed every validation step required by the health plan's operating procedure.

Read the entire response, not only its first sentence.
Confirm material conditions and disclosure language.
Use an authoritative source when required by policy or when confidence is reduced.
Do not silently choose between conflicting sources.
If the response cannot be validated, use the approved fallback or escalation process.
Record or communicate information only through approved systems and channels.

9. Responsible Use, Feedback, and Issue Reporting
9.1 Responsible use
Use CSR Chat only for approved work and supported benefit inquiries.
Enter only PHI or PII permitted by the health plan's approved policy and workflow.
Do not copy, export, print, or share responses unless the action is explicitly permitted.
Do not use CSR Chat as a substitute for plan documents, legal determinations, claims adjudication, or coverage decisions.
Do not use unsupported domains or roadmap features as though they are available.
Report suspected inaccurate information, privacy concerns, or security issues through approved channels.

12. FAQs, Current Limitations, and Glossary
12.1 Frequently asked questions
Question
Answer
Does CSR Chat replace benefit documents or authoritative systems?
No. It assists the representative and does not replace required sources, procedures, or human judgment.
Are CSR Chat responses always correct?
No. Every response requires representative review and any validation required by the health plan.
What information does CSR Chat use?
Approved benefit data associated with the plan context supplied for the request. Exact sources are deployment-specific.
Can I enter PHI or PII?
Only when and how the health plan's approved privacy, security, and operating procedures permit it.
Can I use CSR Chat for claims, eligibility, formulary, or provider questions?
Only if that information domain has been explicitly enabled and validated for your deployment. These are not baseline capabilities.
What should I do if an answer looks wrong?
Do not communicate it. Verify against an authoritative source and use the approved feedback or escalation path.
How do I get technical help?
Use the customer-designated support channel and include only permitted diagnostic information.
