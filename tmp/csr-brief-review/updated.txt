[033] Heading1 | 1. Product Snapshot
[034]  | Compass CSR Chat is an AI-powered decision-support product for health plan customer service representatives. During a live member or provider interaction, a representative asks a benefit question in natural language and receives a structured, plan-specific response grounded in the health plan's approved benefit data. The product is designed to reduce the effort required to locate and interpret benefit information while preserving human review and established customer procedures.
[035]  | PRODUCT PROMISEHelp representatives find, interpret, and communicate plan-specific benefit information faster and more consistently, while keeping the representative accountable for the final customer communication.
[036] Heading2 | 1.1 What Compass CSR Chat is
[037]  | Compass CSR Chat brings benefit interpretation into the representative workflow. It retrieves the plan context supplied with the request, identifies relevant benefit information, and generates an answer using that context. When the available context does not support an answer, the product is designed to say that it cannot answer rather than rely on general knowledge.

[054] Heading2 | 1.3 Product scope
[055]  | The current product scope is representative-assisted benefit inquiry. Supported plan populations depend on which benefit data has been integrated and validated for the health plan. Additional domains are enabled only after their source data, rules, and validation are complete.
[056] ListBullet | Agent-facing: Compass CSR Chat supports representatives; it is not a member-facing chatbot.
[057] ListBullet | Decision support: It assists with benefit questions; it is not a system of record or claims adjudication system.
[058] ListBullet | Plan-grounded: It answers from approved customer context; it is not permitted to answer from general knowledge.
[059] Heading1 | 2. Problem and Value
[060] Heading2 | 2.1 The customer service challenge
[061]  | Benefit inquiries create a time-sensitive knowledge problem. Representatives may need to navigate multiple plan documents, internal tools, and policy sources while a member or provider waits, then interpret specialized language and communicate the applicable conditions consistently. The result can be avoidable search effort, variable interpretation, and limited visibility into recurring knowledge gaps.
[062]  | Challenge
[063]  | Operational effect
[064]  | Manual navigation across sources
[065]  | Longer time to locate and interpret the relevant benefit detail
[066]  | Complex plan language
[067]  | Dependence on individual experience and benefit literacy
[068]  | Inconsistent interpretation
[069]  | Variation in responses, disclosures, and escalation behavior
[070]  | Context switching
[071]  | Higher representative effort and reduced confidence during live calls
[072]  | Limited visibility
[073]  | Difficulty identifying recurring gaps and improvement opportunities
[074] Heading2 | 2.2 How Compass CSR Chat helps
[075]  | Compass CSR Chat creates a controlled path from approved benefit data to a representative-ready response. The application uses the supplied health plan and plan context, retrieves relevant benefit information, and generates a structured answer for representative review. Customer-specific configuration controls terminology, presentation, and required disclosure language. Unsupported questions are declined when the approved context cannot support an answer.
[076] Heading2 | 2.3 Expected outcomes
[077] Heading3 | Value hypothesis
[078]  | Compass CSR Chat is intended to improve representative efficiency and consistency without transferring decision accountability to the AI. The value case should be measured against each health plan’s baseline before and after deployment.
[079] ListBullet | Faster access to benefit information during live interactions.
[080] ListBullet | More consistent interpretation and presentation across representatives.
[081] ListBullet | Greater representative confidence when handling complex benefit questions.
[082] ListBullet | A repeatable validation and feedback loop for improving answer quality.
[083] ListBullet | A scalable foundation for additional plans and approved information domains.
[084]  | MEASUREMENT APPROACHBaseline and target measures are established with each health plan before launch. Recommended measures include median time to answer, validated-answer accuracy, required-disclosure compliance, representative adoption, feedback rate, unsupported-question rate, and benefit-inquiry handle time. Results depend on source-data quality, implementation scope, workflow design, and adoption; the brief does not commit to unvalidated performance targets.

[145] Heading1 | 4. Use Cases
[146] Heading2 | 4.1 Benefit coverage
[147]  | A representative asks whether a service is covered under the applicable plan. Compass CSR Chat returns the available plan-specific coverage detail and relevant conditions for review.
[148]  | Illustrative question: “Is physical therapy covered under this plan?”
[149] Heading2 | 4.2 Cost sharing and conditions
[150]  | A representative asks about copays, coinsurance, deductibles, visit limits, network tiers, or other conditions contained in the loaded benefit data.
[151]  | Illustrative question: “What cost sharing applies to an in-network specialist visit?”
[152] Heading2 | 4.3 Plan interpretation
[153]  | A representative asks for the applicable benefit language to be summarized in a consistent, customer-approved answer structure, including relevant limitations or disclosure language.
[154]  | Illustrative question: “What limitations apply to this durable medical equipment benefit?”
[155]  | Use-case boundary
[156]  | Illustrative questions do not guarantee that every plan or information domain is available. A use case is supported only when the required source data and rules are in scope and have passed customer validation.

[188] Heading1 | 6. Responsible Use and Boundaries
[189]  | These boundaries define the conditions under which Compass CSR Chat should be positioned and used. They protect the distinction between decision support and authoritative systems or determinations.
[190] ListBullet | The representative remains responsible for following approved procedures and communicating the final answer.
[191] ListBullet | Compass CSR Chat does not replace formal plan documents, legal determinations, claims adjudication, or coverage decisions.
[192] ListBullet | The calling application supplies the applicable customer and plan context; Compass CSR Chat does not independently identify a member or determine which plan applies.
[193] ListBullet | Questions outside the available approved context are not answered.
[194] ListBullet | Customer-specific privacy, security, retention, and PHI requirements are governed by the applicable agreements and the Technical Architecture & Security Overview.
[195] ListBullet | Roadmap capabilities must not be represented as available until their source data, integration, controls, and validation are complete.
