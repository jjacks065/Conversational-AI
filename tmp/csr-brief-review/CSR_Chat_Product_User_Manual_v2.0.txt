1. Introduction
Compass CSR Chat, is a customer-service software capability that enables customer service representatives and other authorized agents to work with an AI-powered assistant. Its purpose is to help agents locate, synthesize, and review health-plan-related information while handling customer inquiries.
Human accountabilityCompass CSR Chat assists the agent in locating and understanding information. It does not replace required business processes, authoritative source documentation, or human judgment. The agent remains responsible for following applicable procedures and validating information before communicating it to a customer.

1.1 Intended Audience
The expected primary audience is the front-line customer service representative or authorized agent. 
1.2 What This Manual Covers
The product concept and role of AI
Candidate categories of information Compass CSR Chat may use
A safe, general pattern for asking questions and reviewing responses
Provisional workflow templates for common health-plan inquiries
Responsible-use principles and validation points
Known evidence gaps that must be resolved before publication
1.3 What This Manual Does Not Cover
Formal benefit interpretation policy
Claims adjudication or authorization processing
Legal, privacy, regulatory, or compliance requirements not established by approved materials
Undocumented product controls, integrations, error messages, service levels, or escalation paths
2. Product Concept and Workflow

3. Information Sources
AtlasIQ is the primary information source for Compass products.  Data within the platform will include health plan benefit, network, demographic and other data sourced directly from health plan sources.
3.1 Benefit Documents
Compass CSR Chat may retrieve information that is sourced from health plan benefit document data, Evidence of Coverage data, and related benefit references. These sources may describe covered services, cost sharing, limitations, exclusions, and plan rules.
3.2 Provider and Network Information
Compass CSR Chat may retrieve information from network or provider databases to support provider-network questions.
3.3 Plan, Product, and Internal Knowledge
Compass CSR Chat may use plan and product reference information, internal knowledge sources, and other approved health-plan or customer-service data sources.

9. Review and Validate
When relying on a Compass CSR Chat response, agents should be prepared to note the following parameters that determine a complete answer:
Correct member and plan context
Benefit category and service description
Network tier and provider participation
Cost share, deductible, limitations, exclusions, and authorization conditions
Effective dates and plan year
Applicable source document and section
Conflicts between Compass CSR Chat and another authoritative system
Do not treat AI output as finalA concise or confident answer can still be incomplete, outdated, inapplicable, or misunderstood. Use approved  systems and source documents as a second verified source, as required.

12. When Compass CSR Chat Cannot Answer
Do not guess or fill gaps on Compass CSR Chat's behalf. The approved manual must specify the operational response for each condition below.
Condition
Safe interim direction pending validation
No answer or information not found
Rephrase once if the question may be ambiguous, then use the approved authoritative system or support path.
Source unavailable
Do not rely on an unsupported statement. Use the approved source or fallback process.
Sources conflict
Do not silently choose. Identify the conflict and apply documented source precedence or escalate.
Response appears incorrect
Stop, verify in authoritative sources, and report through the approved feedback or incident process.
Response is incomplete
Ask a focused follow-up or use another approved information source.
Network result is unclear
Verify provider identity, location, specialty, plan/network, and source freshness.
Application error
Preserve allowed diagnostic details and follow the approved technical support process.
