4. Use Cases
4.1 Benefit coverage
A representative asks whether a service is covered under the applicable plan. Compass CSR Chat returns the 
available plan-specifitc coverage detail and relevant conditions for review.
Illustrative question: “Is physical therapy covered under this plan?”
4.2 Cost sharing and conditions
A representative asks about copays, coinsurance, deductibles, visit limits, network tiers, or other conditions 
contained in the loaded benefitt data.
Illustrative question: “What cost sharing applies to an in-network specialist visit?”
4.3 Plan interpretation
A representative asks for the applicable benefitt language to be summarized in a consistent, customer-
approved answer structure, including relevant limitations or disclosure language.
Illustrative question: “What limitations apply to this durable medical equipment benefitt?”
Use-case boundary
Illustrative questions do not guarantee that every plan or information domain is available. A use case is 
supported only when the required source data and rules are in scope and have passed customer 
validation.
